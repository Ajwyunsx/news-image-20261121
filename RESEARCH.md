### 4. Science / space
**BepiColombo arrives at Mercury**
- The ESA–JAXA stack was scheduled to perform Mercury Orbit Insertion on 21 November,
  after eight years in space and six Mercury flybys. MPO's chemical thrusters handle
  the capture; the two orbiters separate on 9–10 December; science starts April 2027.
- Only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury.
