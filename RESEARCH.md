## Lead — Climate / Europe

**COP31 runs past its deadline as the fossil-fuel words vanish**
- COP31 in Antalya, Turkey opened on 9 November and was due to close on Friday 20 November
  at 18:00. It did not. COP31 president Murat Kurum, Turkey's environment minister,
  adjourned the plenary with a promise of a third revision overnight; a new, possibly final
  text was expected on Saturday 21 November.
- The second draft, published Thursday, removed every mention of coal, oil and gas — at
  least 23 countries objected — and deleted the $120bn-a-year adaptation-finance target in
  favour of a promise to "at least triple adaptation finance by 2035". Detail was pushed
  into an "Antalya-to-Addis Ababa Roadmap on Adaptation and Resilience Finance", with a
  report due at COP32 in 2027.
- The draft still states adaptation needs at $305bn a year, retains a "35 by 35"
  electrification target (35% of energy from clean sources by 2035 — 75 countries back it),
  and leans on carbon capture and storage, which critics call a licence to keep burning
  fossil fuels. For the first time a COP text concedes that 1.5°C will be overshot.
- France and Panama led the pushback. Panama's environment minister Juan Carlos Navarro:
  "It is not acceptable to have a COP or a cover document that does not even mention fossil
  fuels, and we simply will not put up with it."

## Sidebar 2 — Europe / Ukraine

**Another night of drones, and a strike on a power site**
- Overnight into 21 November Russia fired three Iskander-M/KN-23/S-400 ballistic missiles
  and 148 drones, 87 of them jet-powered. Ukraine's air force says 117 were shot down or
  suppressed; strikes were recorded at 19 locations.
- Kryvyi Rih, Zelensky's hometown, took the worst of it: a 20-year-old ambulance paramedic
  and a seven-year-old boy were killed, 58 people injured including six children, and some
  25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools, a restaurant and garages damaged.
- Eleven people, two of them children, were wounded when a drone hit a five-storey
  apartment block in the Kharkiv region. One person was hurt in Kyiv.
- Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland; the prime minister
  called it a likely provocation timed to European military exercises.

## Grid 4 — Science / Space

**BepiColombo begins its arrival at Mercury**
- The ESA–JAXA stack starts Mercury orbit insertion on 21 November with the first of a
  sequence of 16 manoeuvres, after eight years in space and nine gravity assists. The two
  orbiters — ESA's MPO and JAXA's Mio — stay stacked until 9–10 December; science begins
  April 2027. Only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury.
- Sources: ESA "Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury"
