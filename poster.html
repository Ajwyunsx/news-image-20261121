Middle East · Conflict

Houthi missiles reach
Riyadh’s skyline

Saudi air defences intercepted three ballistic missiles over the capital for a third night running, after the Saudi-led coalition struck Hodeidah and Sanaa. The Houthis answered by threatening Jeddah’s port.

Americas · Markets

AI trade cracks and the Nasdaq loses its 2026 gains

The index fell 5.3% to 22,617.73 — its worst week in six years. Nvidia slid 14% after an SEC accounting report; the S&P 500 and Dow both entered correction.

The Dow lost 8% on the week, its worst since March 2020, and the S&P 500 shed 8.6%.

■Bitcoin broke below $60,000 and the VIX jumped 26% to 36 as risk appetite drained away.
Europe · Climate

Antalya talks run past their deadline

COP31 entered overtime after a draft deleted every reference to fossil fuels and a $120bn adaptation target. A third text was due on Saturday.

Nearly 200 delegations must still adopt a cover decision.

■France and Panama led calls to restore the fossil-fuel language.
Asia · Diplomacy

Xi leaves Shenzhen holding the stronger hand

Trump called the summit a win, but analysts say Beijing kept the trade truce exactly where it likes it — unresolved, and renewable on its terms.

The truce expires on 10 January; an extension is expected at the G20 in Miami next month.

■China is on track for its soybean pledge; rare-earth shipments remain far below pre-war levels.
Science · Space

BepiColombo slips into orbit at Mercury

After eight years and six flybys, Europe and Japan’s paired orbiters began the first survey of the planet since Messenger. Science starts in April.

It is only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury, and the first with two spacecraft at once.

■The UK-built MIXS instrument has already caught X-rays from the Crab Nebula on the way in.
Africa · Society

Mlambo-Ngcuka,
1955–2026

South Africa’s first woman deputy president, later head of UN Women, died at 71. The UN, the ANC and UNAIDS led the tributes.

She held the deputy presidency from 2005 to 2008 and led UN Women from 2013 to 2021.

■UNAIDS called her “a fearless feminist leader” who put women at the centre of the HIV response.
Americas · Sport

Antonelli fastest, but Verstappen takes pole

A five-place engine penalty dropped the new champion to sixth in Las Vegas. Both Ferraris were out in Q2; Russell starts alongside the Red Bull.

The race itself runs on Saturday night under the lights on the Strip.

■It is the first time no Ferrari has reached Q3 since the Canadian Grand Prix of 2024.
